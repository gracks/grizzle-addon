# "Veilmoor" Realm Patchnotes

# ================================================================================
# v1.2 (10/30/2026)
# ================================================================================

## At a Glance
- Mobs are tougher, hit harder and less predictably, call their friends, and each kind fights its own way. Some wind up big attacks you can dodge with your new Slip (Paladins and Mages get their own button instead)
- The weather matters: rain soaks you, snow slows you, sandstorms blind you, and storms bring their own loot and creatures. A day lasts 2 hours, a year about a month, and nights are darker
- Recovery takes time: sit down, wear Spirit, and rest by a campfire your friends' professions can build up
- Every kill is worth looting, crafted gear is worth making, and rares are worth hunting
- Huge roamers far above the zone's level walk the wilds
- About 9 in 10 open-world quests are gone. In their place: treasure maps, bottles, dig sites, chest finds, salvage, the dead to bury or rob, and XP from gathering, crafting, fishing and first kills
- The world is big again: no flight paths, no minimap, fewer portals, and riding at 40.  Mount speed bonus has been reduced by half.
- Death leaves part of your XP in a tombstone to win back, and a mark from whatever killed you
- The two sides are enemies again, unless a friend from the other side vouches for you

## Mobs Hit Harder
- **Tougher mobs:** normal mobs have 80% more health than stock (was 20%) and deal 15% more damage (was 20%)
- **Low levels hit harder:** up to about 2.5x as hard at level 1, back to normal by level 15
- **Bigger damage swings:** mob hits vary a lot more
- **Crits:** 8% (was 5%), +1% per level the mob is above you, up to +5%
- **Crushing Blows are back:** 4%, +1% per level above you, for 175% damage. They knock your cast back to the start, can throw you off your mount, and sometimes leave a **Deep Wound** (20% less maximum health for 30 minutes) that a good enough bandage treats
- **Thrown from your mount:** any hit for 15% of your health or more throws you off and stuns you for 2 seconds
- **Rotlung:** sickly green mobs (plaguehounds, plagued undead) can give you a cough that slows health and mana regen by 60% for 30 minutes and spreads to party members nearby. Any disease cure ends it
- **Hot Wax:** kobolds splash you with burning candle wax. Getting wet puts it out
- **Poisons** from mobs in the open world do twice the damage
- **Fall damage** starts lower and hits 40% harder

## How Mobs Fight
- **Longer chases:** mobs give up only after 3 minutes without damage
- **More adds:** mobs call for help from a little further, the same kind always help each other, and neutral creatures defend their own
- **Gray mobs leave you alone,** unless you sit down near one
- **Every kind has its own trick (levels 1-40 so far):** wolves howl for the pack, kobolds run for help, boars charge, spiders web you from range, ogres stomp, Defias rogues kick your casts, Frostmane trolls freeze you and run, and much more
- **Attacks you can see coming:** wolves crouch and leap, boars paw the ground and charge, two-handed brutes wind up a Heavy Swing, quilboar bristle their quills. They never miss: step aside, or use Slip
- **Slip:** every character has it at level 1, except Paladins and Mages. For 0.75 seconds you dodge every physical attack. 1-minute cooldown
- **Diminishing returns** on mobs' stuns, fears and roots
- **Camps (Elwynn and Westfall so far):** gnolls and murlocs live in camps around their tents and huts, each with a chest, instead of being scattered over the hills
- **Fewer mobs** where it was crowded (Northshire 30% fewer, the rest of Elwynn and Westfall 20%, Coldridge 25%), and open-world respawns take 25% longer
- **Sneak by night:** at night mobs notice you from 25% closer
- **Safe graveyards:** no hostile mobs within 30 yards of a spirit healer

## Loot and Crafting

### Loot
- Mobs drop crafting materials that fit what they are: beasts their meat, fangs and hides, humanoids cloth every time, elementals their element. Elites drop more, rares always drop the rare stuff
- Those materials sell for less to make up for it, and stack to 40 (or 20)
- **Named mobs** (quest targets like Baron Longshore) always drop one prize: a green, a Rusted Coffer or a trinket, and 3x money. They respawn in 30 minutes
- Rarer crafting reagents drop where they fit: Felcloth from demons, Dark Runes from undead, Breath of Wind from air elementals...
- Humanoids drop food and drink twice as often, and beasts drop meat, sea creatures fish, kodos milk
- **Wanted lists:** one merchant in each town buys three local goods at 3x the price, for a little XP too. The lists change every three days
- **Ore veins** take 2-3 picks again
- **Murky vials:** unlabeled potions from humanoids and chests. Usually a good potion that lasts at least 10 minutes, sometimes something odd, now and then something bad. Alchemists can Assay them first
- **Potions work like vanilla:** a 2-minute cooldown that starts when you drink, not when the fight ends
- World drop rates: greens 1.5 -> 1.2, blues 1.5 -> 1.0, epics 1.5 -> 1.2

### Crafting
- Crafted armor gets **+15% stats and armor**, crafted weapons up to 15% more damage, and all of it is **Bind on Equip** so you can make gear for friends
- **91 new recipes** fill the gaps in Leatherworking, Tailoring, Blacksmithing, Enchanting, Engineering and Inscription
- **11 crafted weapons** have effects now: Rend, poison, slows, an extra attack and more
- **Blacksmithing:** Ice Cleats (boots that stop snow slowing you) and Spiked Shield Rims (hurt mobs that crit you). Mithril Spurs are gone
- **Patterns from the wild:** patterns you can't buy or train drop from mobs of the right level, more often from rares, elites and creatures that fit the recipe

### Rares and Quest Rewards
- **Every rare** always drops one of 3 themed items for its level, often a recipe or potions too, and double money. Each has a signature move, and most now share a spawn point with a normal mob (25% chance it's the rare)
- **Elites** drop the rewards of the quests that send you after them. Farmable elites drop them 10% of the time
- **Normal mobs** can drop the gear rewards of their quests, including the quests that were removed

## Roamers
- **Roamers:** oversized monsters well above the zone's level walk set routes through every leveling zone (elites in Northrend). Spot them before they spot you. Each drops a guaranteed green or blue, a trophy and good money
- **Valley roamers:** one level 12-13 roamer in each starting zone's 5-10 country, each with a big move of its own

## Days, Seasons and Weather
- **2-hour days:** a full day and night take 2 hours instead of 24
- **Weather everywhere,** fitting the land: thunderstorms in jungles and swamps, sandstorms across the deserts, snow in the cold north, fog thick in haunted places and closing in with rain
- **Drenched:** rain or swimming soaks you. Fire hurts you 10% less, frost and nature 10% more, and health regenerates 35% slower. A campfire dries you, and an oiled cloak keeps the worst of it off
- **Charged Air:** thunderstorms make nature damage hurt more, and lightning can strike you in the open
- **Snowbound:** heavy snow slows your movement, attacks and casting
- **Sandblasted:** sandstorms make long shots miss and slow standing mana regen
- **Mobs suffer it too:** drenched, snowbound or sandblasted mobs notice you from closer. Rain is a good time to sneak past
- **Seasons:** a year lasts about a month. Winter brings snow to the temperate north, and the weather in each zone shifts with the season
- **Dark nights:** nights in Elwynn and Duskwood are much darker
- **Fireflies** come out on some evenings in green and tropical zones, most often in summer
- **Weather on your screen:** frost, raindrops, storm dark and blowing sand at the screen's edges

### What Weather and Night Bring
- Spells of the weather's element deal 5% more damage (frost in snow, nature in storms, fire in sandstorms)
- Storms drop elemental materials from kills, grow richer herbs and veins, and bring creatures of their own: Storm Spirits, Snowdrifts, Sand Devils, Rainwalkers
- **Fishing** changes with the weather and the hour (some fish bite only at night or by day). Storms wash up wreckage to fish for, your line sometimes brings up a Waterlogged Satchel, and the town criers hear about trophy catches
- Some herbs loot better at night, and beasts skinned in heavy snow give more hides

## Rest and Recovery
- **Sit to recover:** out of combat, health returns at 30% of its old rate standing and 80% sitting; mana at 30% standing and 80% sitting
- **Spirit matters:** Spirit from gear restores 2.5x as much mana and 60% more health
- **Slower at low levels:** health regen starts at a fifth of normal, mana at half
- **Calm rest:** after 5 minutes out of combat you build rested XP anywhere
- **Cozy Fire:** build a campfire (Cooking) and sit by it for a minute for an hour of +8 Spirit and slower drenching
- **A camp worth making:** a cook's Stew Pot, a leatherworker's Lean-to, a smith's Field Anvil, an alchemist's Cauldron, a tailor's Bedroll (rested XP like an inn) and a first-aider's Dressing Station each add a buff for everyone sitting at your fire
- **Food matters:** a cooked meal gives an hour of Well Fed: more health, and a stat that depends on the dish. Bought and dropped food heals more slowly and gives no buff. Mages no longer conjure food and water
- **Class buffs last twice as long** (Fortitude, Mark of the Wild, Arcane Intellect, Blessings...)

## Travel
- **No flight paths,** no flight masters
- **No minimap:** a compass across the top instead, and the world map no longer shows where you are
- **Fewer portals:** most portals between the capitals, Shattrath and Dalaran are gone. Take the boat, the zeppelin or the road
- **One recall cooldown:** the Hearthstone and every other way home share one 60-minute cooldown
- **Mage portals earlier:** capitals at 30, Darnassus and Thunder Bluff at 40
- **Warlock dens:** summoning works only at a shrine in 22 towns, and needs no helpers there. Meeting stones no longer summon
- **Hired horses:** stable masters rent out a horse by the half hour, from level 1
- **Bindstone (Jewelcrafting 125):** split the stone with a friend, and your half pulls you to whoever carries the other, once an hour
- **Canoe (Leatherworking 100):** paddle the lakes and coasts, with room for a friend
- **Riding at 40 and 60** (death knights included), ground mounts half as fast, and no flying mounts
- **Town criers** in every town share the local news: storms, wanted goods, dangerous sightings and who brought them down

## XP
- XP from kills: 1.3 -> 1.8
- Group bonuses: 2 players 1.25, 3 players 1.45, 4 players 1.65, 5 players 1.85 (was 1.0 to 1.4)
- Rares give 3x XP, and your first kill of each kind of creature gives 3x
- XP from gathering, crafting, fishing, exploring (3x) and your first clear of each dungeon

## Classes
- **Mages:** Glint instead of Slip: for 0.75 seconds physical hits do 80% less, and catching one lets your next spell hit form a Mana Stone. The level 10 class quest teaches Evocation. Each school's first spell comes with a passive: Smolder (Fireball stacks for Fire Blast), Gathering Cold (a stronger Frostbolt the longer you wait) and Brimming Mana (stronger Arcane Missiles above half mana)
- **Paladins:** Ward of Light instead of Slip: for 0.75 seconds physical hits do 80% less, and catching one leaves Lingering Light, a heal that Holy Light or Flash of Light brings in at once

## Death
- **XP loss:** 20% of a level (was 25%), taken when you release, and it can take you down a level (you keep your talents and gear)
- **A lingering soul:** part of the XP you lost waits where you fell. Walk back and claim it. You return to life at the graveyard with full health and mana and no resurrection sickness
- **Shades:** what waits for you is a faint, kneeling copy of you in the gear you died in
- **The mark of your death:** whatever killed you leaves one of 19 marks for an hour, or until you reclaim what you left: Raw Burns from fire, Hamstrung from wolves, Haunted from shadow (no hearthing), and more

## Speech
What happens to you comes through in what you say: a polymorphed player bleats, a silenced one mumbles, a feared one stammers, a cold one's teeth chatter, and a badly hurt one winces.

## Horde and Alliance
- The Horde and Alliance are enemies again: the other side's guards attack on sight. Cross-faction parties and chat still work
- **Vouched for:** with a party member from the other side close by, their people will trade with you, train you and give you quests
- **Shady Dealers** just outside eight towns sell the basics to the other side, and repair
- **Wandering tutors** train Horde classes near Goldshire (1-10) and in Westfall (11-20)
- Hearthstones set at the other side's inns now take you home

## Quests and Finds
- No Dungeon Finder, quest markers, sparkling quest objects, or Find Herbs, Minerals, Treasure and Fish
- **Fewer, better quests:** about 3,900 open-world quests are gone, and every daily and weekly quest outside the Argent Tournament. Each zone keeps its best two storylines per faction and level range. Quests in your log can still be finished
- **Treasure maps and bottles:** riddle maps from bandits and gnolls, or washed up on the shore, lead to buried strongboxes
- **The Legend of Stalvan (Duskwood):** follow Stalvan's own letters and journal instead of the courier chain
- **Dig sites:** eight old excavations with earth anyone can dig
- **Chest finds and early finds:** chests and starting-zone finds hold leads: stolen crates, letters, keys and maps
- **Salvage and Rusted Coffers:** scrap and locked coffers from mobs, worked at an anvil, loom or tanning rack for XP and loot
- **Drying letters:** fished letters come up soaked. Dry them by a fire to read where they lead
- **The dead (Elwynn, Westfall and Duskwood):** lay the remains to rest for XP, or rob them and risk what follows
- **Curios and trinkets:** fun toys and forms, and tradeable trinkets, from rares, chests and strongboxes

## Odds and Ends
- Holidays are off (the Darkmoon Faire and fishing contests stay), and city bells toll at dawn, noon and dusk
- Durotar's colors are softer
- New characters start with food and water
- A corpse's loot window always shows for a moment, even with autoloot
- The Grizzle addon explains Grizzle's buffs and debuffs. Start WoW with the "Play Grizzle" launcher: it keeps the addon and client patch up to date and updates itself

# v1.1 (12/20/2025)

## Combat
- Creature base chance to daze player reduced from 40% to 25% (in practice should be similar to vanilla)

## XP
- XP rate increased from 1.2 to 1.3
- Increased max bonus XP from killing tough enemies from +200% to +500%

## Items
- Elite and boss creatures now additionally drop their associated quest reward items directly. All quest choice rewards drop (instead of picking 1 of 3-6 items). No longer required to do quests for dungeon/raid bosses and rare spawns. 
- Item drop rate for Uncommon/Rare/Epic reduced from 2.5 to 1.5

## Faction
- Alliance players are now allied to most Horde team NPCs and vice versa (a bunch of dangerous exceptions remain sprinkled throughout the world, will try to fix those)
- Players can use the opposing faction's flightpaths, mailboxes, trainers, merchants, and banks

## Professions
- Max professions reduced from 4 to 3

## General
- Talent reset cost is always 10 silver
- Reduced mail delivery time from 60min to 5min
- Removed profession-based stat bonuses that were added post-vanilla
- Hearthstone cooldown increased from 30min to 60min
- Fishing durations restored to vanilla values (WOTLK cut them nearly in half)
- **Fishing works like vanilla again:** you need the zone's fishing skill to catch anything there, and fish get away often until you're well above it (about 95 points over for none; lures and poles help), even in schools. A fish that gets away gives no junk and no skill, and skill rises a little slower. Expert Fishing comes from the book "Expert Fishing - The Bass and You" (Old Man Heming, Booty Bay) and Artisan from Nat Pagle's quest in Dustwallow Marsh; trainers still teach the rest. Ranks you already know stay
- Guild charter signatures requirement reduced from 9 to 1
- Optional XP pause cost reduced from 10gold to 10copper
- Disabled Achievements

# v1.0 (12/14/2025)
# ================================================================================

## Combat
- All normal creatures deal 20% more damage and have 20% more health ("normal" means not Elite or Boss)
- Aggro Range: All units aggro from the same distance regardless of level difference (auras that increase/decrease detection range still work normally)
- Removed the massive regen boost added in WoW live patch 3.3 that affected player levels 1-14
- Glancing Blows have been completely disabled. What it was: When attacking enemies higher level than you, you had a chance to deal "glancing blows" that dealt significantly reduced damage (up to 40% damage reduction).
- Crushing Blows have been completely disabled. What it was: Enemies 4+ levels above you could deal "crushing blows" that dealt 150% damage.

## XP
- Player XP rate increased from 1.0 to 1.2
- Killing enemies higher level than you now grants bonus XP based on the level difference. How it works:
  - For each level the enemy is above you, you get +30% XP
  - Maximum bonus is capped at +200% (~7 level diff)
- Players now lose 25% of their current level's XP when they die.  Cannot lose a level from this.

## Item and Gold Drop Rates
- Item drop rates increased:
  - Normal: 1.0 -> 1.2
  - Uncommon: 1.0 -> 2.0
  - Rare: 1.0 -> 2.5
  - Epic: 1.0 -> 2.5
- Bosses can now drop 2 items from their core loot list instead of 1
- Gold drop rate increased from 1.0 to 1.3

## Reputation Rate
- Reputation gain rate increased from 1.0 to 4.0

## Professions
- Max professions increased from 2 to 4
- Engineer-crafted items no longer require Engineering skill to use

## Horde and Alliance Interactions
- Removed all interaction restrictions between Horde and Alliance characters.  They can now group, trade, chat, add to friends, send mail, inspect, and join same guild.
- Accounts can now have a mix of Horde and Alliance characters on the same server

## QoL
- Logout time (/camp) is instant
